## [0.1.0] - 2026-07-13

### Added

- Initial release: `HakimSTTService` and `HakimTTSService` for Pipecat.
- `HakimSTTService`: realtime transcription over `WSS /v1/audio/transcriptions/stream`.
- `HakimTTSService`: realtime speech synthesis over `WSS /v1/audio/speech/stream`.
